What you actually get

Not a course. A working starter kit.

Four things, in plain terms. No certificate, no subscription, no cut of the money.

1

Your own web address

Something like yourbusiness.hpnextgen.com — short enough to say out loud and type from a phone. No Wix link, no free-host banner.

2

Pricing that holds up

We work through supplies, time and fuel so a $25 job doesn't quietly cost $30 to finish. Flat prices get size and condition limits.

3

A way to count the money

A plain sheet for jobs, expenses and what's actually left. Enough to know whether the business is real.

4

Cards, flyers, QR codes

Printed business cards and a flyer with a scannable code, so a customer can find you again without hunting.

What we don't do: take a cut, own the customer list, or make the business depend on us. If it moves to its own domain later, that's fine.

The rules

What the program asks of you

A service, not an idea

Mowing, cleaning, detailing, hauling, pet care, small repairs — something a neighbor can buy this month.

Transport and supervision

A parent or guardian stays involved. We don't place anyone at a job site.

Proof of the work

Real photos of finished jobs. No stock pictures, no invented reviews on any NextGen site.

Finish what you quote

Show up when you said. If the price was wrong, the customer doesn't eat the difference.
